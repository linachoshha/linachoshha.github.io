What if stories could show us how our minds work?

While working on my memoir - which blends scenes of village life, Oxford, caregiving, and faith - I began to wonder:
What happens inside the brain when we read a story?
Emotionally. Cognitively. Neurologically. 
That question led me to narrative neuroscience, fMRI studies, LLMs, and human-centered AI.

I’m running experiments : 
some technical, some narrative - to trace how stories shape minds.
I’m exploring how meaning might move through time, memory, and the brain.

I’m documenting this journey in public - idea by idea, insights by failures - on [<i class="fab fa-x-twitter"></i> NarrativeMirror](https://twitter.com/NarrativeMirror){:target="_blank"}

If you're curious about:

Human-centered AI
Interpretability
Narrative cognition
Neuroscience and how we process narrative
Meaning-making
Or simply… Authentic, original stories - ones that move, linger, and challenge… I’d love for you to follow, reflect, or reach out.

This is just the beginning.
